About Me

I am a Registered Midwife with a calm heart and a steady hand. At Sara Medical Center I support families through the whole journey of pregnancy — from gentle antenatal care and tailored birth planning to skilled labour support and compassionate postnatal guidance. I blend evidence-based clinical practice with cultural sensitivity and clear communication, because every birth is unique and every family deserves respectful, dignified care. I find deep purpose in empowering parents with education, advocating for safe childbirth, and helping newborns take their very first breaths with confidence and calm.
